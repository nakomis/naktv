# Siraya Tech Test Model V5

- **Model:** Siraya Tech Test Model 2021 v5
- **Uploaded by:** Fluppi, on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5438127
- **Designed by:** Siraya Tech, building on work by Peopoly and Photonster's XP2
  (https://siraya.tech/pages/siraya-tech-test-model)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY). See `LICENSE.txt`, as
  downloaded. Any video or print shown of it must credit the above.

It's here as the subject of NAKTV-21: how much of a printed design can be
rebuilt from the layer images shown on the TV and in the rendered videos.
It's made for judging resolution, with pins, holes and recessed text at
known sizes, which is what that comparison needs. `README.txt` is the
uploader's description, unchanged.
